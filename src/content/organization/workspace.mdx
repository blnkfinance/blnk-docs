Your workspace is the control center for instances and data in Blnk Cloud. Each organization has one workspace. If you belong to more than one organization, you can switch between them without signing out.

Update the current workspace in [Settings > Workspace](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/workspace).

![Workspace settings page showing organization details and the Domains section](img/organization/workspace-domains.png)

You can update the organization name, team size, and country.

## Workspace domains

Verify domains your organization owns so teammates with a matching email address can request to join, without a manual invite.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Domains section">

Go to [Settings > Workspace](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/workspace) and scroll to **Domains**.

</Step>
<Step title="Start adding a domain">

Click **Add domain** at the top right of the Domains section.

![Domains section with the Add domain button highlighted](img/organization/workspace-add-domain-button.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Enter domain details">

In the **Add domain** panel:

- **Domain:** the domain you own, such as `company.com`
- **Verification email:** an address on that domain

Click **Send verification code**.

![Add domain side panel with Domain and Verification email fields](img/organization/workspace-add-domain-panel.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Confirm ownership">

Enter the verification code sent to that email. Once confirmed, the domain appears in the **Domains** tab.

</Step>
<Step title="Review join requests">

Switch to the **Join requests** tab to approve or decline teammates who signed up with an email on a verified domain.

![Join requests tab showing teammates requesting to join the workspace](img/organization/workspace-join-requests.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Domain-based join requests are separate from [team invitations](/access-control/team). Admins can still invite anyone by email from **Settings > Team members**.
</Tip>

## Switch workspace

If you belong to more than one organization, you can move between workspaces without signing out.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your workspace menu">

At the top of the left sidebar, click your workspace name or logo.

![Sidebar workspace control with the workspace menu open](img/organization/switch-workspace-sidebar-menu.png)

Select **Switch workspace**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose a workspace">

Pick the workspace you want to open. Your current workspace is highlighted in the list.

![Switch workspace list showing available workspaces with the current one highlighted](img/organization/switch-workspace-list.png)

You may see a brief loading state while the switch completes.

</Step>
</Steps>

When the switch finishes, the dashboard shows the workspace you selected. If that workspace still needs setup, Blnk Cloud walks you through it before you continue.

<Tip>
If something looks missing after a switch, check that you are in the right workspace and instance. Data does not carry over between workspaces.
</Tip>

## Add a workspace

You can create a workspace for another organization from the same **Switch workspace** menu.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your workspace menu">

At the top of the left sidebar, click your workspace name or logo, then select **Switch workspace**.

![Sidebar workspace menu with Switch workspace option](img/organization/switch-workspace-sidebar-menu.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Create the new workspace">

Click **Add workspace**, then enter the organization name, team size, and country.

![Add workspace form with organization name, size, and country fields](img/organization/add-workspace-form.png)

Click **Create workspace**. Blnk Cloud creates the workspace and opens it.

</Step>
</Steps>
