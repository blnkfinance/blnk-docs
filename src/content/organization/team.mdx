You can invite unlimited teammates to join your workspace and collaborate on your data. Manage them in [Settings > Team members](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/team).

![Team members page showing active teammates and the Add member button](img/organization/team-members-page.png)

## Send an invitation

Only admins can send invitations.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Team members">

Go to [Settings > Team members](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/team).

</Step>
<Step title="Click Add member">

Click **Add member** at the top right of the page.

![Team members page with the Add member button highlighted](img/organization/team-add-member-button.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Enter email and role">

In the **Invite a team member** panel:

- **Email address:** your teammate's email
- **Role:** **Admin**, **Operator**, **Investigator**, or **Analyst**

![Invite a team member panel with email and role fields](img/organization/team-invite-panel.png)

Blnk Cloud has four preset roles: **Admin**, **Operator**, **Investigator**, and **Analyst**. Choose the role that matches what your teammate needs to do.

![Role dropdown showing Admin, Operator, Investigator, and Analyst](img/organization/team-invite-select-role.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Send the invitation">

Click **Send invite**. Your teammate receives an invite link by email with steps to join the workspace.

![Invite a team member panel with email and role filled in and Send invite enabled](img/organization/team-invite-send.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

### Track pending invitations

To see teammates who have not accepted yet, open the **Invite pending** tab on the Team members page.

![Invite pending tab showing email addresses awaiting acceptance](img/organization/team-invite-pending.png)

<Tip>
Teammates with an email on a [verified workspace domain](/organization/workspace#workspace-domains) can request to join without an invite. Review those requests under **Settings > Workspace > Join requests**.
</Tip>

## Roles and permissions

Blnk Cloud has four preset roles. Open the **Roles & permissions** tab on the Team members page to select a role and review what it can access.

![Roles and permissions tab showing Admin, Operator, Investigator, and Analyst](img/organization/team-roles-permissions.png)

| Role | Summary |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Admin** | Full access to back-office, deploy, and monitoring features, including billing and team management. |
| **Operator** | Handles back-office operations and transaction management. |
| **Investigator** | Focused on fraud detection, alerts, and investigations. |
| **Analyst** | Read-only access for data analysis and monitoring. |
