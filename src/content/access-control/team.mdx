Team members are the people who work with you in the organization. Invite them when you want to share instances, ledger data, and back-office work.

Manage them in [Settings > Team members](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/team).

![Team members page showing active teammates and the Add member button](img/organization/team-members-page.png)

## Invite a teammate

Only admins can send invitations. The invite uses a built-in role: **Admin**, **Operator**, **Investigator**, or **Analyst**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Team members">

Go to [Settings > Team members](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/team).

</Step>
<Step title="Click Add member">

Click **Add member** at the top right of the page.

![Team members page with the Add member button highlighted](img/organization/team-add-member-button.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Enter email and role">

In the **Invite a team member** panel:

- **Email address:** your teammate's email
- **Role:** **Admin**, **Operator**, **Investigator**, or **Analyst**

![Invite a team member panel with email and role fields](img/organization/team-invite-panel.png)

Choose the role that matches what your teammate needs to do.

![Role dropdown showing Admin, Operator, Investigator, and Analyst](img/organization/team-invite-select-role.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Send the invitation">

Click **Send invite**. Your teammate receives an invite link by email with steps to join the workspace.

![Invite a team member panel with email and role filled in and Send invite enabled](img/organization/team-invite-send.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Enterprise invite

Verify a domain your organization owns, and people who sign up with that email can ask to join. An admin approves the request, and they become a team member. 

You do not send a separate email invite.

<Steps>
<Step title="Verify the organization domain">

Go to [Settings > Workspace](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/workspace) and scroll to **Domains**.

![Workspace settings page showing organization details and the Domains section](img/organization/workspace-domains.png)

Click **Add domain**.

![Domains section with the Add domain button highlighted](img/organization/workspace-add-domain-button.png)

In the **Add domain** panel:

- **Domain:** the domain you own, such as `company.com`
- **Verification email:** an address on that domain

Click **Send verification code**, then enter the code from that email. The domain appears in the **Domains** tab once it is confirmed.

![Add domain side panel with Domain and Verification email fields](img/organization/workspace-add-domain-panel.png)

</Step>
<Step title="They sign up">

The person signs up with an email on that domain. Blnk invites them to join the organization, and the request waits for an admin.

</Step>
<Step title="Approve the request">

Open **Settings > Workspace** and switch to the **Join requests** tab. Approve the request, and they are in the organization.

![Join requests tab showing teammates requesting to join the workspace](img/organization/workspace-join-requests.png)

</Step>
</Steps>
