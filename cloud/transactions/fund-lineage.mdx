Fund lineage lets you tag money as it enters a balance, then see which providers still have funds available when you spend.

Use it when you need to know not only the balance total, but the mix of sources inside it (for example Stripe vs PayPal).

For how Core tags funds, allocates debits, and stores lineage, read [Fund lineage tracking](/transactions/lineage).

---

## Enable lineage on a balance

Turn lineage on when you create the balance. You cannot add it later to a balance that already exists.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open create balance">
    Go to **Balances** and click **Create balance**, or press `⌥ + B` (`Alt + B` on Windows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on lineage">
    Under **Advanced options**, enable **Enable lineage tracking**.

    Pick an allocation strategy for later debits:

    - **FIFO:** spend the oldest tagged funds first
    - **LIFO:** spend the newest tagged funds first
    - **PROPORTIONAL:** spread the debit across tagged funds

    Cloud also turns on **Assign an owner**. Lineage requires an identity on the balance.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/cloud/img/balances/create-balance-lineage.png" alt="Create balance form with lineage tracking and allocation strategy" className="rounded-lg" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the balance">
    Choose the identity, then click **Create balance**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tag a credit

Set the lineage-enabled balance as the destination first. Cloud only shows **Lineage tag** after that. If the destination does not have lineage, the control stays hidden.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a credit">
    Go to **Transactions** and click **Create transaction**. Set the lineage-enabled balance as the **destination**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a provider tag">
    Under **Advanced options**, turn on **Lineage tag** and enter a provider name (for example `stripe` or `paypal`). Cloud sets `BLNK_LINEAGE_PROVIDER` in metadata.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/cloud/img/transactions/lineage-tag-credit.png"
        alt="Create transaction form with Lineage tag on and Provider set to Stripe"
        className="rounded-lg"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the transaction">
    Complete the amount and source as usual, then click **Create transaction**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you omit the tag, the credit still posts. It does not join lineage tracking.

---

## Spend from a lineage balance

When you spend from that balance, create a normal debit. Leave **Lineage tag** off. Cloud applies the allocation strategy you chose on the balance (FIFO, LIFO, or PROPORTIONAL).

To see how that spend was split, open the debit. Use **Fund lineage** for the amount from each provider, and **Shadow transactions** for the internal rows Core created to track it.

---

## Where to read the breakdown

The **Fund lineage** section appears only when lineage is enabled on that balance or debit. Other balances and transactions stay unchanged.

To see totals, available, and spent per provider, open the lineage-enabled balance. The details page also shows the allocation strategy (for example FIFO).

## Filter lineage balances

On the **Balances** table, add filters for:

- **Track fund lineage** to list balances with lineage on or off
- **Allocation strategy** to list `FIFO`, `LIFO`, or `PROPORTIONAL`

See [Add filters](/cloud/filters/add-filters).
