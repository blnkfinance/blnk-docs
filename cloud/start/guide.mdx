Blnk exists to give developers the tools and confidence to bring any financial product idea to life regardless of whether you're working solo, with a small team, or as part of a large organization.

Blnk Cloud is a clean and intuitive platform that allows you to view and act on your Core's data, collaborate with your team, and monitor your transactions for any anomalies in real-time.

This guide will walk you through getting started with Blnk Cloud and help you get set up quickly.

## 1. Sign up or log in

To access Blnk Cloud, visit [cloud.blnkfinance.com](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com).

<img src="/cloud/img/start/signup-login.png" alt="Blnk Cloud sign-up and login page" className="rounded-lg" />

For first-time users, create a new account. You can sign up via Google or manually provide your account details.

We send a 6-digit verification code to your registered email address to verify your account. Once your email is verified, you'll be redirected to sign in with your account information.

> For existing users, sign in via Google or with your email and password.
> 
> <Tip>Google SSO is automatically enabled for your account if your email is linked to a Google account.</Tip>

## 2. Workspace setup

To complete your account, you need to create and set up your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organization">

    Enter your organization's information: 
    - Organization name
    - Team size 
    - Country
    - Team

    <img src="/cloud/img/start/02-workspace-setup.gif" alt="Setting up a Blnk Cloud workspace with organization details" className="rounded-lg" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Cloud plan">
    Select a Cloud plan that fits your needs to complete your setup and enter your workspace. 

    Choose between our 2 plans:

    - **Sandbox:** Shared infrastructure for development, testing, and smaller low-risk workloads. Build and validate your money movement and ledger logic before going to production.

    - **Production:** Dedicated infrastructure for live workloads. Includes everything in Sandbox plus automated backups, high availability, and dedicated support.

    <img src="/cloud/img/start/cloud-plans.png" alt="Comparison of Blnk Cloud plans: Sandbox and Production" className="rounded-lg" />

    <Info>To learn more about our plans, visit our [Pricing page](https://blnkfinance.com/pricing).</Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Deploy a sandbox

For Cloud to work, you need a running Blnk Core instance. Deploy a Sandbox to start recording transactions with no manual setup.

<img src="/cloud/img/start/03-deploy-instance.gif" alt="Instance creation form showing the Sandbox deployment type and pricing summary" className="rounded-lg" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the instance form">
    Click **Deploy an instance** from your homepage, or go to **Settings → Instances**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sandbox and deploy">
    Toggle **Deploy Managed Core**, select **Sandbox**, and name your instance. Review the pricing summary and click **Deploy instance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait until it's connected">
    Sandbox instances typically deploy within 7 - 10 minutes. When the connection status updates to **Connected**, you can start using your instance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Self-hosting your Core?" icon="cloud" href="/cloud/instances/create">Self-hosted Core connected to Cloud.</Card>

## 4. Create your first transaction

Once your sandbox is connected, record a transaction in Cloud. Every transaction needs a source and a destination. Use internal balances (names that start with `@`) so Blnk creates them for you in the [General Ledger](/cloud/ledgers/general-ledger).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create transaction form">
    Go to **Transactions** and click **Create transaction**, or press `⌥ + T` (`Alt + T` on Windows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the transaction details">
    Use these values:

    - **Amount:** `10.00`
    - **Currency:** `USD`
    - **Precision:** `100`
    - **Source:** `@FundingPool`
    - **Destination:** `@MyBalance`
    - **Description:** `My first Blnk transaction`

    Enable **Apply overdraft**. New balances start at 0, so `@FundingPool` needs an overdraft for this first transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the transaction">
    Click **Create transaction**. The new transaction appears in the Transactions table. Open it to confirm the source, destination, and amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You've recorded your first transaction in Blnk Cloud.
</Check>
