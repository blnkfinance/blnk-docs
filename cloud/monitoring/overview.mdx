Monitoring is a dedicated view of operational activity in your Cloud workspace. Use it to scan recent requests and status without leaving the dashboard.

<img src="/cloud/img/monitoring/monitoring-dashboard.png" alt="Cloud monitoring dashboard" className="rounded-lg" />

Open **Monitoring** from the sidebar. Review timestamps, status codes, and items that need attention, then jump to the related instance or record when you need more detail.

For ledger risk events (flagged balances, assigned work), use [Alerts](/cloud/alerts/overview) instead.
