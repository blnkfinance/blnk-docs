Notification settings control **how and when Blnk emails you**. Open them from **Settings > Notifications**.

Today the page covers **Deployment anomalies**. Other email types (login, instance created, weekly ledger summary) are not on this screen.

<img src="/cloud/img/settings/notification-settings.gif" alt="Open Settings, Notifications, and configure deployment anomaly email thresholds" className="rounded-lg" />

---

## Deployment anomalies

Cloud emails you only after a burst of matching anomaly detections. Below your threshold, nothing is sent.

Defaults: **3** detections within **30** minutes.

| Field | What it does | Limits |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **No of detections** | How many matching detections must occur before an email is sent | 1 to 100 |
| **Duration** | Rolling window those detections must fall in, in minutes | 5 to 1440 |

---

## Daily summary

Turn on **Send me a daily recap of recurring anomalies** if you want a digest in addition to threshold emails. It is on by default.

Click **Save changes** when you are done.
