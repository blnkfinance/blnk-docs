## How plans work

We offer two billing options: monthly and yearly (yearly saves 10%).

Plan fees are billed in advance based on your billing frequency. Additional usage is calculated at the end of each month:

- On monthly plans, additional usage is billed with the next month's plan fee.
- On yearly plans, the plan fee is billed annually in advance. Additional usage is billed monthly as a separate usage charge.

Refer to our [pricing page](https://blnkfinance.com/pricing) to learn more about our plans and view pricing comparison.

## Usage-based billing

Usage-based billing ties your Cloud cost to the size of your ledger. As your product generates more ledger activity, your record count grows.

Each Sandbox and Production deployment includes a record allowance. When a deployment stores more records than its allowance, additional usage charges apply.

### What counts as a record?

A record is a ledger object created within your instance. Records include:

- Ledgers
- Transactions
- Balances
- Identities
- Reconciliations

| Object type | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Ledgers | 10 |
| Balances | 10 |
| Transactions | 100 |
| Identities | 5 |
| Reconciliations | 0 |
| **Total records** | **125** |

In this example, the instance contains 125 records.

### How billing works

| Instance type | Included records | Additional usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sandbox | 100,000 | `$0.000135` per record |
| Production | 200,000 | `$0.000175` per record |

At the end of each monthly billing cycle, Blnk calculates usage from the total records stored in each instance. This includes records from previous months, not only records created during the current month.

When the total exceeds the included allowance, you are billed for the additional records at the rate shown above. See the [Pricing page](https://blnkfinance.com/pricing) for current rates.

### Example

A Sandbox instance contains 150,000 records.

| Description | Records |
| --- | --- |
| Included records | 100,000 |
| Additional records | 50,000 |

The additional usage charge is 50,000 × `$0.000135`, or **`$6.75`**. This is added to the Sandbox subscription fee.

Additional records remain stored in the instance and keep counting toward your bill for as long as they remain stored there. Destroying or terminating the instance removes those records from your usage total.

### Understanding record growth

Your record count grows as your application creates transactions, balances, identities, and reconciliation records.

For capacity planning, you can estimate future record growth using:

- Expected transaction volume
- Expected balance creation
- Expected identity creation
- Expected reconciliation activity
- Historical record growth

### Monitor usage

Use the usage information in Cloud to track:

- Total records
- Included record allowance
- Records above allowance
- Invoice summary

## Manage your billing

To manage your billing, go to [Settings > Billing](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/billing) to access your billing information and make changes.

From the billing settings page, you can:

### View your active subscriptions

See a full breakdown of your active subscriptions and deployments, including product details, status, billing frequency, next payment date, and monthly amounts.

To update your billing address or payment method, click `Edit` on either the billing address or payment method card to update your information.

You can also click on `Manage subscription` to update other subscription details.

## Change or cancel your subscription

Admins can change or cancel their subscription from the Billing page. Subscription updates will take effect immediately.

If you're upgrading, we charge the prorated amount of your new plan for your billing period. If you're downgrading, we charge nothing.

We do not offer refunds for cancellations mid-subscription. When you cancel, your subscription will remain active until the end of your billing period.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I was charged twice when creating my subscription.">
We will initiate a refund for the duplicate charge. This may take up to 5 - 7 business days to process depending on your bank.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What counts as a record?">
Any ledger, transaction, balance, identity, or reconciliation created within your instance counts as a record. Your record count is the total number of these objects stored in the instance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How is usage billed?">
Each deployment includes a maximum number of records. When you exceed the included allowance, you are billed monthly for the additional records stored in that deployment.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do additional usage costs reset every month?">
No. Additional records remain stored in the instance and keep counting toward your bill for as long as they remain stored there. Destroying or terminating the instance removes those records from your usage total.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan limits?">
Your instance continues running normally. Additional charges apply for records stored beyond your plan's included allowance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I run my product with Sandbox?">
Yes. Sandbox is suitable for building, testing, and smaller low-risk workloads. It does not include SLA-backed guarantees such as automated backups or high availability. For those guarantees, use a Production instance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="When should I upgrade to Production?">
Upgrade to Production when you need dedicated infrastructure, high availability, automated backups, compliance, or guaranteed operational and support SLAs. See [Sandbox vs Production](/cloud/instances/overview#sandbox-vs-production).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Will my invoice increase as my business grows?">
Yes, but predictably. Your bill is tied directly to records stored, so it grows with your additional usage. You can estimate costs by checking your record count.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
