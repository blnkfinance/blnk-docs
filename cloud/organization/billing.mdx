Billing is where you manage your organization's plan, invoices, payment details, and record usage. To manage your billing, go to [Settings → Billing](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/billing).

## Usage-based billing

Sandbox and Production instances include a usage limit measured in records.

A record is any ledger, transaction, balance, identity, or reconciliation external data stored in an instance. Your record count is the total of these objects, including records from previous months. 

**Why records?** We measure usage this way so your Cloud cost only grows as your product grows.

<img src="/cloud/img/organization/billing-usage.png" alt="Billing Instance usage table with a tooltip breaking down records into ledgers, balances, transactions, identities, and reconciliations" className="rounded-lg" />

When you reach your included limit, you choose whether to opt into usage-based billing:

- If you opt in, you can keep creating records. Blnk charges you for each extra record stored, not for each extra record created during the month.
- If you don't opt in, you cannot create new records in the instance.

At the end of each monthly billing cycle, Blnk takes a snapshot of the records stored in each instance. Extra records above your allowance are billed from that snapshot. 

- On monthly plans, this is billed with the next month's plan fee. 
- On yearly plans, it is billed monthly as a separate usage charge.

<Note>
Extra records stay in the instance and keep counting toward your bill until you destroy or terminate the instance.
</Note>

## Manage your billing

From [Settings → Billing](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/billing), you can update your plan and payment details, review subscriptions and invoices, or cancel your subscription.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Manage your subscription">
    Click **Manage subscription** to change your plan, billing address, or payment method. Plan updates take effect immediately. 
    
    - If you're upgrading, we charge the prorated amount of your new plan for your billing period. 
    - If you're downgrading, we charge nothing.

    <img src="/cloud/img/organization/billing-manage-subscription.png" alt="Billing page Plan card showing the Manage subscription button" className="rounded-lg" />

    When a payment fails, you'll no longer have access to your Cloud workspace until you resolve the pending invoice. Update your payment details to resolve the pending invoice.

    <Note>
    If you don't resolve the payment within 30 days, any active deployments will be deleted and your subscription will be automatically cancelled.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View subscriptions">
    See your active subscriptions and deployments.

    <img src="/cloud/img/organization/billing-subscriptions.png" alt="Billing page showing active subscriptions, billing frequency, next payment date, and monthly amounts" className="rounded-lg" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="View invoices">
    View and download your past invoices.

    <img src="/cloud/img/organization/billing-invoices.png" alt="Billing page invoices table showing invoice number, description, date, amount, and payment status" className="rounded-lg" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel your subscription">
    Click **Manage subscription** to cancel. We do not offer refunds for cancellations mid-subscription. 
    
    When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of your billing period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Is Blnk Cloud a fully-hosted solution?">
Yes. You can deploy a fully managed ledger from your Blnk Cloud workspace with one click. No extra setup required. See [Managed instances](/cloud/instances/deploy).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How is storage measured?">
Storage refers to the number of records stored. A record is a ledger, transaction, balance, identity, or external data uploaded for reconciliation in your instance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How is usage billed?">
Each instance includes a maximum number of records you can store. When you go over the limit, you can opt into usage-based billing. You are then billed monthly for the additional records stored in that instance, not for records created during the month.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Will my invoice increase as my business grows?">
Yes, but predictably. Your bill is tied directly to records stored, so it grows in line with your additional usage. You can estimate costs anytime by checking your record count.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do additional usage costs reset every month?">
No. Your additional records remain stored in the instance and keep counting toward your bill until you destroy or terminate the instance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I run my product with Sandbox?">
Yes. Sandbox works for smaller, low-risk projects. It does not include SLA-backed guarantees such as backups or 99.9% uptime. For those guarantees, use Production. See [Deployment types](/cloud/instances/overview#deployment-types).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="When should I upgrade to Production?">
Upgrade to Production when you need dedicated infrastructure, high availability, backups, compliance, or guaranteed operational and support SLAs.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is there a free trial for Blnk Cloud?">
Yes. New customers get a 5-day trial on the Sandbox plan, including a sandbox instance to build your product.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan limits?">
If you opt into usage-based billing, your instance keeps running and additional charges apply for records stored beyond your included amount. If you don't opt in, you cannot create new records in the instance.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens if a payment fails?">
You no longer have access to Blnk Cloud until you resolve the pending invoice. If you don't resolve it within 30 days, any active deployments will be deleted and your subscription will be automatically cancelled. See [Manage your subscription](#manage-your-billing).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I migrate from another platform to Blnk?">
Yes. If you need help with migration and onboarding, [contact Support](mailto:support@blnkfinance.com). To move data between self-hosted Core and Cloud, see [Migrate your instance](/cloud/instances/migration).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I get started?">
Sign up to Blnk Cloud. Every organization starts on Sandbox, with a 5-day trial. Then deploy an instance or connect your self-hosted Core. See [Getting started](/cloud/start/guide). If you run into issues, [contact us](mailto:support@blnkfinance.com).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
