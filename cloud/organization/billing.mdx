Billing is where you manage your organization's plan, invoices, payment details, and record usage.

Open [Settings → Billing](https://cloud.blnkfinance.com/cloud/settings/billing) to view and update them.

<img src="/cloud/img/organization/plan-comparison.png" alt="Comparison of the Open-source, Sandbox, and Production plans with current monthly prices and included features" className="rounded-lg" />

We offer two billing options: monthly and yearly (yearly saves 10%). Refer to our [pricing page](https://blnkfinance.com/pricing) to learn more about our plans and view pricing comparison.

---

## Usage-based billing

Usage-based billing ties your Cloud cost to the records stored in each instance. A record is any ledger, transaction, balance, identity, or reconciliation in that instance. Your record count is the total of these objects, including records from previous months.

Each Sandbox and Production instance includes a record allowance:

| Instance type | Included records |
| --- | --- |
| Sandbox | 100,000 |
| Production | 200,000 |

At the end of each monthly billing cycle, Blnk takes a snapshot of the records stored in each instance. Records above your allowance are billed as additional usage. On monthly plans, this is billed with the next month's plan fee. On yearly plans, it is billed monthly as a separate usage charge.

Your instance keeps running if you exceed the allowance. Additional records stay in the instance and keep counting toward your bill until you destroy or terminate the instance.

## Monitor usage

The **Instance usage** table shows:

- **Name** and **Type** (Sandbox or Production)
- **Usage**: records stored against your included allowance (e.g., 138 / 100K)
- **Extra records**: records stored beyond your included allowance
- **Usage cost**: the charge for those extra records

## Manage payment details

Click **Manage subscription** to update your billing address or payment method.

---

## Change your subscription

Click **Manage subscription** to change your plan. Updates take effect immediately. If you're upgrading, we charge the prorated amount of your new plan for your billing period. If you're downgrading, we charge nothing.

---

## Cancel your subscription

Click **Manage subscription** to cancel. We do not offer refunds for cancellations mid-subscription. When you cancel, your subscription will remain active until the end of your billing period.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I was charged twice when creating my subscription.">
We will initiate a refund for the duplicate charge. This may take up to 5 - 7 business days to process depending on your bank.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Should I use Sandbox or Production?">
It depends on your infrastructure needs, not your record volume. See [Sandbox vs Production](/cloud/instances/overview#sandbox-vs-production).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Will my invoice increase as my business grows?">
Yes, but predictably. Your bill is tied directly to records stored, so it grows with your additional usage. You can estimate costs by checking your record count.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
