Insights is the SQL workbench for your ledger history. On a managed Sandbox or Production instance, it reads the instance **data lake**, a stored copy of your ledgers, balances, transactions, and identities.

Use it to report on volume, status mix, wallets, and identities. 
To call the same lake SQL from your backend, use the [Data lake API](/cloud/reference/data-lake-api).

## Open Insights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Insights">
    Navigate to the side navigation and open `Insights`.

    <Note>
      **Note:** Make sure you're in the right instance where you want to query the lake. If you're not sure, you can confirm by checking the active instance in the top right corner of your screen.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a query">
    Click `New query` to open a blank SQL editor. Until you save, the draft is named Untitled lake query.

    The left sidebar has three tabs:

    - **[Queries](/cloud/insights/queries):** reopen Saved queries, manage Scheduled runs, and check History.
    - **[Schema](/cloud/insights/schema):** browse the tables and columns you can query.
    - **[Templates](/cloud/insights/templates):** open a starter query and edit it for the report you need.

    <img src="/cloud/img/insights/workbench-new-query.png" alt="Insights workbench after New query, with Untitled lake query and the Queries tab open" className="rounded-lg" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the range, run, and save">
    Pick start and end dates in the toolbar. Keep the range as tight as the question allows. See [How the lake works](/cloud/insights/lake) for why the date range matters.

    Write a `SELECT` or `WITH` query, or paste a [template](/cloud/insights/templates). Click **Run** (or ⌘ `Enter` / `Ctrl` `Enter`). Results can be a **Table** or a **Chart**. Click **Save** if you want it on the instance. For schedules and history, see [Queries](/cloud/insights/queries).

    <img src="/cloud/img/insights/run-and-save.gif" alt="Set the date range, run a query, then save it from Actions" className="rounded-lg" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
