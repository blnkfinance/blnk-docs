Blnk Cloud requires a running Blnk Core instance to work. You can [host your own Core instance](/cloud/instances/create) or deploy a managed Core instance from Blnk.

For managed instance, Blnk takes care of the deployment and maintenance of your Core instance, and automatically connects it to your Blnk Cloud workspace.

---

## Deployment types

Managed instances come in two types:

- **Sandbox:** Shared infrastructure for small or low-risk workloads. No uptime or data-retention SLA.
- **Production:** Dedicated infrastructure with high availability, backups, region selection, and dedicated support.

Here's a quick comparison:

|  | Sandbox | Production |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Best for | Small or low-risk workloads | Large or high-risk workloads that need operational guarantees |
| Infrastructure | Shared | Dedicated |
| Included records | 100,000 | 200,000 |
| Throughput | Up to 5,000 TPM | Up to 100,000 TPM |
| Uptime SLA | <Icon icon="x" size={16} color="#DC2626" /> | 99.9% |
| High availability and backups | <Icon icon="x" size={16} color="#DC2626" /> | <Icon icon="check" size={16} color="#16A34A" /> |
| Multi-region support | <Icon icon="x" size={16} color="#DC2626" /> | <Icon icon="check" size={16} color="#16A34A" /> |
| IP whitelisting | <Icon icon="x" size={16} color="#DC2626" /> | <Icon icon="check" size={16} color="#16A34A" /> |
| Built-in data lake | <Icon icon="x" size={16} color="#DC2626" /> | <Icon icon="check" size={16} color="#16A34A" /> |
| Support | Standard support | Dedicated Slack and 24/7 SEV 1 response |

## Deploy a managed core instance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an instance on Cloud">
    1. Click **Deploy an instance** from your homepage. 
    2. Alternatively, go to **Settings → Instances** and click **Deploy an instance**.

    <Frame caption="Click Deploy an instance from your homepage, or from Settings → Instances">
    <img src="/cloud/img/instances/managed-instance-open-form.gif" alt="Clicking Deploy an instance to open the instance creation form" className="rounded-lg" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your deployment type and deploy">
    Follow the steps for your preferred [deployment type](#deployment-types).

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Sandbox">
        1. Select **Sandbox** as your deployment type.
        2. Enter a descriptive name for your instance, e.g. "blnk-sandbox-staging".
        3. Review the pricing summary.
        4. Click **Deploy instance** to start the deployment.

        <Frame caption="Select Sandbox, name your instance, review pricing, and deploy">
        <img src="/cloud/img/instances/managed-instance-form-sandbox.gif" alt="Instance creation form showing the Sandbox deployment type and pricing summary" className="rounded-lg" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Production">
        1. Select **Production** as your deployment type.
        2. Enter a descriptive name for your instance, e.g. "blnk-production-staging".
        3. Choose a region closest to your app for your instance.
        4. Review the pricing summary.
        5. Click **Deploy instance** to start the deployment.

        <Frame caption="Select Production, name your instance, choose a region, review pricing, and deploy">
        <img src="/cloud/img/instances/managed-instance-form-production.gif" alt="Instance creation form showing the Production deployment type, region selection, and pricing summary" className="rounded-lg" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to connect">
    Sandbox instances typically deploy within 7 - 10 minutes; Production instances can take up to 10 - 30 minutes.

    When it's ready, the connection status updates to **Connected**. Click the instance from your homepage, or go to **Settings → Instances** and click it, to open the details panel. 
    
    Use the details to get your Core URL and secret key for Core API requests.

    <Frame caption="Click an instance from the homepage or Settings → Instances to open its details panel">
    <img src="/cloud/img/instances/instance-details-view.gif" alt="Instance details panel showing instance details" className="rounded-lg" />
    </Frame>
    
    For Production, you'll also be invited to a dedicated Slack channel. Check your email for the invite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How long does a managed instance take to deploy?">
Sandbox typically takes 7 - 10 minutes. Production can take 10 - 30 minutes. When it's ready, the connection status updates to **Connected**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I stay on Sandbox permanently?">
Yes. Sandbox is a deployment type, not a time-limited trial. Stay on it as long as shared infrastructure without an SLA meets your needs. See [Deployment types](#deployment-types).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do I need Production once I exceed my Sandbox allowance?">
No. Record volume does not require Production. When you reach your included allowance, opt into [usage-based billing](/cloud/organization/billing#usage-based-billing) to keep creating records. 

Choose Production when you need dedicated infrastructure, high availability, backups, or support SLAs.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I move my data off a managed instance?">
Yes. You can export a managed instance to your own infrastructure, or import from self-hosted Core into Cloud. See [Migrate your instance](/cloud/instances/migration).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
