Blnk Cloud requires a running Blnk Core instance to work. You can [host your own Core instance](/cloud/instances/create) or deploy a managed Core instance from Blnk.

For managed instance, Blnk takes care of the deployment and maintenance of your Core instance, and automatically connects it to your Blnk Cloud workspace.

---

## Deployment types

Blnk offers two deployment types for managed Core instances:

- **Sandbox:** Shared infrastructure for building, testing, and smaller low-risk workloads. Includes 100,000 records. Sandbox does not include uptime or data-retention SLAs.
- **Production:** Dedicated infrastructure for workloads that require high availability, automated backups, region selection, and dedicated support. Includes 200,000 records.

Additional records beyond your plan's included allowance are billed under [usage-based billing](/cloud/organization/billing#usage-based-billing).

For a full comparison, see [Sandbox vs Production](/cloud/instances/overview#sandbox-vs-production).

## Deploy a managed core instance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an instance on Cloud">
    1. In your Blnk Cloud workspace, go to **Settings → Instances**;
    2. Click **Deploy an instance**;
    3. You'll see the instance creation form. 

    <Frame caption="Go to Settings → Instances and click Deploy an instance">
    <img src="/cloud/img/instances/managed-instance-open-form.gif" alt="Opening Settings → Instances and clicking Deploy an instance to reach the instance creation form" className="rounded-lg" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your deployment type and deploy">
    Toggle **Deploy Managed Core**, then follow the steps for your [deployment type](#deployment-types).

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Sandbox">
        1. Select **Sandbox** as your deployment type and enter a descriptive name for your instance (e.g., "Staging").
        2. Review the pricing summary and click **Deploy instance**.

        <Frame caption="Select Sandbox, name your instance, review pricing, and deploy">
        <img src="/cloud/img/instances/managed-instance-form-sandbox.gif" alt="Instance creation form showing the Sandbox deployment type and pricing summary" className="rounded-lg" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Production">
        1. Select **Production** as your deployment type, enter a descriptive name for your instance, and choose a region.
        2. Review the pricing summary and click **Deploy instance**.

        <Frame caption="Select Production, name your instance, choose a region, review pricing, and deploy">
        <img src="/cloud/img/instances/managed-instance-form-production.gif" alt="Instance creation form showing the Production deployment type, region selection, and pricing summary" className="rounded-lg" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I created a transaction, but it's not showing up in my workspace.">
  Records may take a few seconds to show up in your workspace. Please refresh and check again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I export my data out of Blnk Deploy?">
  Yes. You can migrate from Blnk Deploy to your own infrastructure or connect your own analytics/backup tools. Only admins can generate secure credentials to access or download data.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I stay on Sandbox permanently?">
  Yes. Sandbox is a deployment option, not a trial. Stay on it as long as it meets your operational and infrastructure requirements.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do I need Production once I exceed my Sandbox allowance?">
  No. Exceeding your plan's included allowance bills additional records under [usage-based billing](/cloud/organization/billing#usage-based-billing). Choose Production when you need dedicated infrastructure, high availability, backups, or support guarantees.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
